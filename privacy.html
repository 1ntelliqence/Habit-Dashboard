Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Habit Dashboard is a personal productivity application used to track habits, reminders, daily details, notes, and journal entries.

Information the app uses

The app may store information you enter, including habit activity, checklist items, notes, preferences, statistics, and journal content.

Google Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, Habit Dashboard uses Google Drive access only to save, retrieve, synchronize, restore, and back up Habit Dashboard data in your own Google Drive account.

Habit Dashboard does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Local storage

Some application data and preferences are stored locally in your browser or device so the app can function offline and remember your settings.

Journal encryption

Journal content may be encrypted before storage. Your journal password is not stored by the app. If you forget that password, encrypted journal content may not be recoverable.

Data sharing

Habit Dashboard is not designed to sell, rent, or commercially share your personal data. Data is transmitted only as needed to provide features you choose to use, such as Google Drive synchronization.

Data retention and deletion

You control the data stored in your browser and Google Drive. You may delete local browser data, remove Habit Dashboard files from Google Drive, or revoke the app's Google account access at any time.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect app data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Habit Dashboard's features or data handling practices change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the application owner through the contact information provided on the Google OAuth consent screen.
